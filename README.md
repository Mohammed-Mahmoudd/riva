## `◈` &nbsp; Overview

> **RIVA** is a premium accessories e-commerce platform curated for the modern woman. From delicate necklaces and handcrafted bracelets to statement rings and elegant hand fans — every piece is selected with intention.

The platform delivers a refined, feminine shopping experience — organized by category, designed for ease, and built around a single idea: that the right accessory completes everything.

## `◈` &nbsp; Design Philosophy

```
  SOFTNESS.       Femininity without compromise.
  CURATION.       Every piece earns its place.
  WARMTH.         A store that feels like a boutique.
  INTENTION.      Detail is the whole point.
```

RIVA's identity is built around the idea that accessories are personal — intimate, expressive, and chosen with care. The platform reflects that: warm tones, editorial layouts, and a browsing experience that feels closer to a curated boutique than a generic storefront.
